Privacy Policy

What information is handled when you visit Old PC Guide and how to contact us about privacy.

About this site

Old PC Guide is an informational website hosted with GitHub Pages. Its domain and DNS are managed through OVH.

The site does not provide user accounts, registration, a newsletter, a members area or online purchases. It does not currently use Google Analytics, Google AdSense, advertising cookies or site-owner tracking tools.

Calculator data

The hardware calculator runs in your browser.

Values you enter, such as processor, memory and graphics card, are used locally by the page to calculate estimates. The calculator does not send those values to us or store them on a site account.

Hosting and technical data

When a website is requested, the hosting and network providers may process technical information needed to deliver and protect the service.

GitHub Pages serves this site's files. OVH manages the domain's DNS. These providers may process connection or diagnostic data under their own terms and privacy notices. We do not operate the hosting infrastructure and cannot specify or control the providers' logging and retention practices.

Email and external websites

If you email us, your email address and the information in your message will be processed so we can read and reply.

Please avoid sending sensitive information. Contact us at info@oldpcguide.com for questions about privacy or to make a request concerning a message you sent.

This site may link to third-party websites. Following a link takes you to that provider, whose own privacy practices apply. We do not control those sites.

Cookies and future changes

The site currently does not set cookies for analytics, advertising or tracking.

Technical processing by the hosting provider may be outside our control. If we add services such as Google Analytics or AdSense, or other technologies that store or access information on your device, we will update this notice and add a Cookie Policy and any consent controls required before enabling them.

This notice may be updated if the site's features or services change. Last updated: October 14, 2026.
